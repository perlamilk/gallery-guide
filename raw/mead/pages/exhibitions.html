On View Now

The Juncture: Ukrainian Artists in Search of Modernity and Identity

May 24–October 13, 2024: This exhibition showcases the work of three leading modern artists from Ukraine who produced work during an astonishing period of the country’s cultural renaissance in the early twentieth century.

Learn More!

Rotherwas Room

The Rotherwas Room, (built England, 1611) is always on view at the Mead, and open as a study spot, an area for classes visiting the museum to convene, and available to community partners upon request. Contact MeadEdu@amherst.edu for more information.

Upcoming Exhibitions

Part of an Impossible Task

Aug. 27, 2024 – Jan. 5, 2025: Part of an Impossible Task presents work by Michael Rakowitz that examines the role of cultural artifacts on how individuals and societies define power, interrogating both history and its retelling.

Learn More

Re/Presenting: Art Beyond the Color Line (PREVIEW)

Aug. 27–Oct. 13, 2024: An interactive, all-ages sneak peek of Re/Presenting, focusing on key works and creative activities from the full exhibition (opening Nov. 12). 

Learn More

Past Exhibitions

To learn about previous Mead exhibitions, visit the Exhibition Archive.

Liliana Porter: Two Realities

Liliana Porter: Two Realities (Spring 2022) foregrounded Argentinian-born, U.S.-based artist Liliana Porter’s decades-long consideration of “two realities”— “virtual reality” (a depiction of a thing) and the “real thing.”

Learn More!

The Living Room

The Living Room (Spring 2021) was curated by DeLyna Hadgu, then a senior and Advanced Student Museum Educator at Amherst College, in collaboration with the Black Art Matters Festival. The show explored how Black artists represent Black subjects.

Learn More! Take the Virtual Tour!

Founding Narratives

Founding Narratives (Spring 2021) presented artworks produced in the United States between 1800 and today, offering opportunities to consider the role of art in creating, reinforcing, and challenging stories about national identity.

Learn More! Take the virtual tour!

Embodied Taste

Embodied Taste (Fall 2020) was the culmination of a student exhibition seminar on food, culture, art, and power, producing an immersive exploration of food and how it moves—through our bodies and our world.

Learn More! Take the virtual tour!
