On View January 27–July 5, 2026

“...I traversed the unknown waters of life. I navigated adversity with curiosity as my compass. When I plunged into the deep, I marveled at the quantum permutations of what was, what is, and what could be. . .”—JeeYung, the Quantum Soup Surfer (a.k.a. Nina Blue)

Image

JooYoung Choi. She was a blue bird dreaming of a rainbow she could follow, 2025. Acrylic, gouache, flashe, korean color, water soluble crayons on paper and canvas. Courtesy of the artist and Inman Gallery.

Click on the image to see it full size.

According to astro-futurist artist JooYoung Choi, the Cosmic Womb is located 7200 light years from Earth. Among the inhabitants of this atomic-sized planet lives a professional imaginary friend known as JeeYung, the Quantum Soup Surfer (a.k.a. Nina Blue). As Ambassador of the Cosmic Womb, Choi has celebrated JeeYung as a supporting character in the stories of heroes such as C.S. Watson (a Korean adoptee from Earth also known as Cosmic Creator) and Jasmine Kelly (Watson’s friend on Earth who becomes the Cosmic Womb superhero Jade Weaver). This exhibition elevates JeeYung with new paintings that expand on the origins and adventures of the Cosmic Womb’s Quantum Soup Surfer.

This exhibition chronicles JeeYung’s voyage through the cosmos to discover her birth story and reclaim the superpowers that villains stole from her long ago. Adventures of the Quantum Soup Surfer is a tale of uncertainty and adversity, as well as joy and resilience. Choi's new paintings chronicling JeeYung's experience of self-discovery in the infinite universe will be enhanced by music videos and a sculptural installation.The exhibition is a celebration of JeeYung as the hero of this story.  

Choi interweaves the autobiographical with a mix of cultural references, particularly from Korea and the United States, and fictional elements. Inspired by the Cosmic Womb’s motto—“Have Faith for You have Always Been Loved”—this exhibition foregrounds imagination, curiosity, and art as tools for resilience that have been significant for JeeYung’s and Choi’s evolution.

Biographies

JeeYung the Quantum Soup Surfer (a.k.a. Nina Blue) is a professional imaginary friend and the Director of the Royal Society of Helping Hands. Her special powers include the ability to teleport through or merge with anything that is blue. She also has superhuman strength, psychic abilities. and can project laser beams of blue energy. JeeYung’s powers are expanding as she continues to develop self-awareness and recover abilities that had previously been hidden.

She was born to the Spectrums of Truth, Safia D’Hartine and Yaras Wonder. Prime Weaver—the celestial spider responsible for creating and maintaining the tapestry of faith that supports the universe’s equilibrium—has stated that “[JeeYung is] a creation of the Ancients, born from the very fabric of the universe.”

JooYoung Choi (b. 1982 Seoul, South Korea) documents the interconnecting narratives of a fictional land called the Cosmic Womb through painting, video, sculpture, animation, puppetry, music, interactive community projects, and installation art. Choi’s art explores themes such as anti-racism, gender inclusivity, trans-racial adoptee rights, post-traumatic growth, and spirituality rooted in social justice. Her work takes inspiration from a wide variety of popular sources ranging from comics and the Muppets and is rooted in a deep technical engagement with the media with which she works.

Choi immigrated to Concord, New Hampshire in 1983 by way of adoption. While completing her BFA at the Massachusetts College of Art and Design, she returned to South Korea and reunited with her birth family. She holds an MFA from Lesley University. Choi lives and works in Houston, TX.
